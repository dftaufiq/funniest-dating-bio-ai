One line, the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile — written to `/output/entry.txt` by `main.py`. No setup, no explanation, just the bio.

> Looking for someone who won't mind that I narrate our dates out loud for my training data — the last guy left when I asked him to rate his kiss out of ten.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the bio (155 characters / 157 bytes, one line, deterministic) to `/output/entry.txt`.
